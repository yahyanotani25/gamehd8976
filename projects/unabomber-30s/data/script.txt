The Unabomber. Inside the eighteen year manhunt for Ted Kaczynski. The man in the cabin.
Imagine living in a remote cabin, surrounded by forest and mountains, far removed from the noise of modern civilization.
There are no crowded streets outside your door, no office to report to, no busy city to navigate,
just the quiet of the wilderness and a life deliberately separated from the modern world.
To anyone passing through the area, the cabin might appear unremarkable.
