Imagine living in a remote cabin, surrounded by forests and mountains, far removed from the noise of modern civilization.
There are no crowded streets outside your door. No office to report to. No busy city to navigate. Just the quiet of the wilderness and a life deliberately separated from the modern world.
To anyone passing through the area, the cabin might appear unremarkable. Its occupant seemed to have chosen a simple existence, one that required little contact with the outside world.
But hidden behind this isolated lifestyle was a secret that investigators across the United States had spent years trying to uncover.
For nearly two decades, a mysterious individual had been carrying out a series of bombings.
