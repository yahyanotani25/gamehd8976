Smart Money Concepts ko samajhna chahte hain?
Order Block woh zone hota hai jahan institutions market mein entry le sakte hain, jis ki wajah se price mein reaction aane ka imkaan hota hai.
Jab yeh block fail ho jata hai aur apna role reverse kar leta hai, toh yeh Breaker Block ban jata hai.
Order Block potential entry ka signal deta hai, jabke Breaker Block potential reversal ko show karta hai.
In concepts par mastery hasil karein aur zyada behtar trading decisions lein!
