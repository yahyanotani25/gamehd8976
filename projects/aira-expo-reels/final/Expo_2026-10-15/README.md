# AIRA expo, 15 Oct 2026: 15 text-free Reels

These reels are cut from the first Drive link (`Raw file.zip`, 142 clips shot on 15 Oct
2026). They follow the concept the client approved from sample Reels 3 and 4, with
**no on-screen text**, as the client asked.

## Flow of every reel

1. **Outside.** The venue entrance and the AIRA campaign standees, opening with the
   reference's light-band intro.
2. **Coming inside.** The expo hall.
3. **The AIRA logo.** The wall letters, cut on the music's drop.
4. **The shop.** The AIRA stall: racks, counter and staff.
5. **People and products.** Guests, the host and visitors in complete looks, mixed with
   close-ups of garments, embroidery, tags and AIRA packaging.
6. **End.** The AIRA wall in full, then the flare across the letters, the eclipse ring,
   the AIRA logo card (silent), and a fade to black, as in the reference.

The grade, transitions (light-leak burn, photo card, whip, zoom, flash, B&W beat flash)
and logo ending are the same as in the approved samples.

All 15 use different music: Mixkit tracks under the Mixkit Stock Music Free License,
which allows commercial use in Instagram and Facebook posts and ads.

## Notes

- Each reel shows at least 2 complete looks. The tracker lists them.
- No shot shows another brand's product legibly. Clips with other brands' bags, perfume
  boxes or sample cards were left out.
- Many guests appear in more than one reel, because one event's footage is shared across
  15 reels. No two reels have the same sequence, and every reel has its own music.
