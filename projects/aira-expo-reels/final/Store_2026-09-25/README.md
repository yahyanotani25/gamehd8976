# AIRA store evening, 25 Sep 2026: 15 text-free Reels

These reels are cut from the second Drive link: the AIRA store event of 25 Sep 2026,
filmed on a DJI Pocket (vertical 3K and 4K, 60 fps) and a Sony A7 III (4K, 30 fps).
They follow the concept the client approved from sample Reels 3 and 4, with
**no on-screen text**, as the client asked.

## Flow of every reel

1. **Outside.** The AIRA campaign standee at the gate and the welcome board, opening
   with the reference's light-band intro.
2. **Coming inside.** The welcome board, the flower-lined staircase, the doorway.
3. **The AIRA logo.** The story wall, the logo above the door, the lit logo wall or the
   red AIRA sign, cut on the music's drop.
4. **The store.** The gold shelves and rails, guests browsing.
5. **People and products.** Guests and hosts in complete looks, mixed with close-ups of
   prints, embroidery and finishing details on the rails.
6. **End.** The lit AIRA wall in full, then the flare across the letters, the eclipse
   ring, the AIRA logo card (silent), and a fade to black, as in the reference.

The grade, transitions and logo ending are the same as in the approved samples and the
expo batch.

## Colour

The store was lit with very warm LEDs on gold shelves, so the camera files are deep
orange and the garments lose their true colours. Each clip is white-balanced on its
own before the look is applied. It goes most of the way to neutral and keeps the
evening's warmth. White walls are never pushed cooler than neutral.

the Mixkit Stock Music Free License, which allows commercial use in Instagram and
Facebook posts and ads.

## Notes

- Each reel shows at least 2 complete looks. The tracker lists them.
- No shot shows another brand legibly. Left out: guests' designer bags, a camera-brand
  microphone, perfume-brand shelves, a bottled-food shelf, the sign of another business
  over the stairs and the night store front, other shops' street signs and a parked car.
- Wall wording that belongs to the store (the "It began with a little girl…" story wall,
  the welcome board) appears where it is part of the shot. Nothing is added on screen.
- Many guests appear in more than one reel, because one evening's footage is shared
  across 15 reels. No two reels have the same sequence, and every reel has its own music.
