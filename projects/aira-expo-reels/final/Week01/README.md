## Matching the reference

- **Opening.** The reference's film-gate light band opens into the first shot.
- **Grade.** The reference's warm grade, measured from matching frames, with its
  vignette and fine grain.
- **Transitions.** Light-leak burn, photo-card, whip pans, zoom blur, echo stutter and
  the B&W halftone beat flash. Cuts land on the beat.
- **Ending.** The AIRA wall, then the flare streak across the letters, the eclipse ring,
  the AIRA logo card in silence (as in the reference), and a fade to black.

## To confirm with the client

1. **Logo.** The logo on the end card and in the hook is traced from the client's own
   reference end card, with the same proportions and colours. Please send the official
   logo file (SVG or PNG) if one exists, and it will be swapped in.
2. **Font.** AIRA's brand font wasn't supplied. Jost, a clean geometric sans that
   matches the logo, was used. Please confirm it, or send the brand font.
3. **Event name.** Two expos ran at the venue: the *Dhaka Fashion & Lifestyle Expo 2026*
   and the *Real Estate & Abasan Expo 2026*. The event isn't named on screen or in the
   captions. If AIRA exhibited at the Dhaka Fashion & Lifestyle Expo, the name can be
   added.
4. **Guest outfits.** The host's and guests' outfits are described only as looks, and
   the captions don't claim they are AIRA pieces. Please confirm which outfits are AIRA
   pieces, so captions can name them.
5. **Footage.** Google Drive stopped allowing downloads of the 25 GB footage zip ("quota
   exceeded") before 8 clips could be fetched at full resolution: C0123, C0124, C0125,
   C0126, C0128, C0131, C0139 and C0142. They contain the rust-kurta guest, the
   bridal-red and dusty-rose saree looks, and a gold-embroidery close-up. The reels use
   other shots instead, and every reel still meets the requirements. If those clips are
   shared again (or once the quota resets), re-running the build swaps the planned
   shots back in automatically.
