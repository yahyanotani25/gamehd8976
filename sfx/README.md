# Sound effects library

The owner's own sound effects, saved for future edits. Use one only when a
video calls for it — none of these belong in every edit.

| File | Length | What it is | Timing | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `click-double.mp3` | 0.65 s | Two sharp clicks, like a camera shutter or mouse click-clack | Hits at 0.16 s and 0.25 s | Text pops, freeze frames, UI or menu moments, small cut accents |
| `whoosh.mp3` | 2.1 s | Air whoosh that swells up and tails off | Loudest at 0.89 s | Transitions, zoom punch-ins, fast pans, swipes |
| `low-drone.mp3` | 35.7 s | Low tonal hum around 180–210 Hz, slowly shifting pitch | Silent for the first 5.3 s; sound runs about 5.3–32.9 s | Tension under an intro, slow-motion build-ups, the calm before a drop |

## Notes for mixing

- Place `whoosh.mp3` so its peak (0.89 s in) lands exactly on the cut.
- Place `click-double.mp3` so its first hit (0.16 s in) lands on the moment.
- `low-drone.mp3` is very quiet (about −34 LUFS, peak −22 dBFS, against
  −17 LUFS for the whoosh). Trim the leading silence and raise its gain
  before using it, then keep it under the music.
- All three are MP3, stereo. The click is 48 kHz; the whoosh and drone are
  44.1 kHz. Resample to the project rate when mixing.
